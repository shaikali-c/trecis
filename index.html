Get to know us

Trecis Consulting Pvt Ltd is a consulting firm specialising in talent acquisition, recruitment and psychometrics. We work with organisations to address hiring requirements while bringing research-informed perspectives to people-related decisions.

We believe successful organisations are built not only on skills and experience, but also on understanding people, their behaviours and the environments in which they work.

“Better people decisions begin with understanding the individual, the role and the wider organisational context.”
